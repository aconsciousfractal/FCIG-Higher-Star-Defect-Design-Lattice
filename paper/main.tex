\begin{abstract}
For a $t$-design $D$ with point-by-block incidence matrix $A$ we study the
\emph{selected higher-star defect}
$\Delt(D)=\Hk/\im_\ZZ(A)$, where $\Hk=\{z\in\ZZ^P:\sum z\equiv 0\ (\mathrm{mod}\ k)\}$
and $k$ is the block size. With $d=\gcdop\{\lambda_r-\lambda_{r+1}\}$ over the
star vector, we prove the sandwich $d\Hk\le\im_\ZZ(A)\le\Hk$, so $\Delt(D)$ is a
finite abelian group killed by $d$, on at most $v-1$ generators, of order
dividing $d^{v-1}$; it is the torsion of the retained-apex cokernel and fits a
canonical sequence $0\to\Delt(D)\to\coker(A)\to\ZZ/k\to0$. A pointed
presentation identifies $\Delt(D)$ with the cokernel of an explicit
$v\times b$ matrix whose Gram matrix depends only on the ordinary design
parameters, yielding a square-order bound and a bilinear discriminant form into
which the dual defect embeds isotropically. Analyzing that form prime by prime
gives: a complete good-prime decomposition and, at odd valuation one, a Witt
obstruction that strictly beats square divisibility on an infinite family of
$S(2,5,v)$; an exceptional-prime rank-three reduction; and, at the prime two in
higher valuation, a metabolicity theorem showing the growing constituent is
always split, so no binary analogue of the odd-prime \emph{discriminant-form}
obstruction grows with $v$ (other binary obstructions are not excluded). We give fixed-parameter phenomena (a valuation-two formula, a same-parameter
separation of two Steiner triple systems, and the complete $\STS(15)$ spectrum),
a local Pasch transport law, an elementary realization theorem, and geometric
applications. Classical ingredients are attributed throughout; no priority or
firstness is claimed. The finite controls are reproduced by an included script.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The companion repository \path{FCIG-Higher-Star-Defect-Design-Lattice} contains
this \LaTeX{} source (main text and appendices), the compiled title-named PDF,
and a single self-contained verifier \path{scripts/verify.py} that constructs the
finite controls from scratch with no external data---the $\SQS(8)$ defect
$(\ZZ/2)^{3}$, the same-parameter $\PG(3,2)$/Bose separation, and the M\"obius
plane $M(2)$---computing each Smith form with \texttt{python-flint}, enforcing an
independent modular cross-check, and asserting byte-identity against the frozen
certificate \path{certificates/expected_verification.json}. The output is
byte-identical under \texttt{python} and \texttt{python\ -O}. Classical inputs are
attributed in \path{docs/SOURCE_LOCK.md} and the bibliography; the claim ledger
and scope boundary live in \path{docs/}. See \path{REPRODUCE.md}.
