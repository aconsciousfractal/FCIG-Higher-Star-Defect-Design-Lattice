\begin{abstract}
For a nonempty $t$-$(v,k,\lambda_t)$ design with $2\le t\le k<v$, let $A$ be
its point-by-block incidence matrix. We study the finite quotient
$\Delt(D)=H_k/\im_\ZZ(A)$, where $H_k$ consists of the integer point vectors
whose coordinate sum is divisible by $k$. A pointed presentation embeds the
dual quotient isotropically in a bilinear discriminant group determined by
the design parameters. This gives an odd-prime bound that improves square
divisibility by one generator on an infinite family of $S(2,5,v)$, together
with a rank-three reduction at exceptional primes. At the prime two, a
general bilinear lemma shows that isotropy gives no further bound on the
order. We also prove that a Pasch switch preserves the odd-primary quotient
and changes its order by at most a factor of two. Finally, published
exact-rank bounds and an explicit asymptotic comparison realize every fixed
elementary binary or ternary group at all sufficiently large orders in the
corresponding Steiner triple-system families. Classical incidence identities,
Smith forms and enumeration results provide the inputs; four finite examples
are reproduced by a companion verifier.
\end{abstract}
