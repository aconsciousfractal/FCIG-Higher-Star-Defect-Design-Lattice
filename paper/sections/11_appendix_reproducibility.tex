\section{Reproducibility of the finite controls}\label{sec:app-repro}

The verifier \path{scripts/verify.py} constructs the following four designs
from scratch, without external data. The middle column gives the full
incidence Smith form; the last column gives the selected quotient.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Design & Incidence Smith form & $\Delt(D)$ \\
\midrule
$\SQS(8)=\AG(3,2)$ & $1^4 2^3 4$ & $(\ZZ/2)^3$ \\
Point--line $\PG(3,2)$ & $1^{11}2^3 6$ & $(\ZZ/2)^4$ \\
Bose $\STS(15)$ & $1^{14}3$ & $0$ \\
M\"obius plane $M(2)$ & $1^4 3$ & $0$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Smith forms are computed with \texttt{python-flint}. A separate modular
implementation checks the binary and ternary ranks of the pointed
presentation. The output is checked against the frozen certificate
\path{certificates/expected_verification.json} and is byte-identical under
\texttt{python} and \texttt{python -O}.

The other cited finite data are imported: the larger M\"obius examples
\cite{Ringe2008}, the projective-plane Smith forms \cite{ChandlerSinXiang},
the Hermitian incidence divisors \cite{Hiss}, and the intermediate
$\STS(15)$ ranks \cite{TonchevWeishaar,ShiXuKrotov}. They are not recomputed
by this verifier.

The finite computations support the two-system comparison in
Theorem~\ref{thm:sep} and the worked example in Appendix~\ref{sec:app-sqs}.
The general arguments for the star bound, pointed presentation, local
bilinear analysis, Pasch transport and elementary realization are written
proofs, using the classical inputs cited in the text. Finite checks do not
establish their uniform conclusions.
