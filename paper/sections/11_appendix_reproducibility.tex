\section{Reproducibility of the finite controls}\label{sec:app-repro}

The self-contained finite controls---the $\SQS(8)$ defect $(\ZZ/2)^{3}$ with
form $1^{4}2^{3}4$; the same-parameter $\PG(3,2)$/Bose separation, forms
$1^{11}2^{3}6$ and $1^{14}3$ with defects $(\ZZ/2)^{4}$ and $0$; and the
smallest M\"obius plane $M(2)=3\text{-}(5,3,1)$ with form $1^{4}3$---are
reproduced from scratch by \texttt{scripts/verify.py}, which computes each Smith
form with \texttt{python-flint} and cross-checks the binary and ternary ranks by
an independent modular route; its certificate is byte-identical under
\texttt{python} and \texttt{python -O}. The other finite forms cited in the text
(the M\"obius ladder for $q\ge3$~\cite{Ringe2008}, the $\PG(2,4)$ valuation-two
form and the general projective calibration~\cite{ChandlerSinXiang}, the
Hermitian divisors~\cite{Hiss}, and the intermediate $\STS(15)$ ranks obtained
by Pasch transport of Theorem~\ref{thm:pt}) are imported or follow from the
stated results, not recomputed here. The general theorems of the main text---the
sandwich, the pointed presentation, the prime-by-prime analysis, the transport
law, and the M\"obius/Hermitian applications---use no finite census; the finite
inputs are confined to the same-parameter separation (Theorem~\ref{thm:sep}) and
the worked $\SQS(8)$ example (Appendix~\ref{sec:app-sqs}).
