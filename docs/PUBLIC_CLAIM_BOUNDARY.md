# Public claim boundary

- The invariant studied is `Delta(D) = H_k / im_Z(A)` and its pointed cokernel
  presentation. Claims concern the **image (column) lattice**; the relation
  kernels `ker_Z(A)` are not part of any claim.
- Discriminant-form isotropy statements are **bilinear**; no `2`-adic quadratic
  (Arf/oddity) refinement is asserted.
- Realizability is claimed only for the elementary `(Z/2)^a` and `(Z/3)^b`
  targets on Steiner triple systems. Nonsquarefree exponents and general
  defect-group realizability are out of scope.
- **No priority, novelty, or firstness** is asserted for any statement. Where
  the paper says no exact prior equivalent was located, this is a dated negative
  search, not a certificate. The classical inputs are the property of their
  authors (Wilson; Caggegi–Falcone–Pavone; Chandler–Sin–Xiang; Ringe; Wall;
  Nikulin; Hanani; Hiss; Assmus–Key; Solé) and are used only as attributed prior
  art.
- The paper carries no electrical, network, or Smith-chart interpretation; the
  "higher-star" terminology refers to the star vector of a design.
