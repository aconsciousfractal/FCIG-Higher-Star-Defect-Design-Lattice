# Public claim boundary

- The invariant studied is `Delta(D) = H_k / im_Z(A)` and its pointed cokernel
  presentation. Claims concern the **image (column) lattice**; the relation
  kernels `ker_Z(A)` are not part of any claim.
- Discriminant-form isotropy statements are **bilinear**; no `2`-adic quadratic
  (Arf/oddity) refinement is asserted.
- At two the generic bilinear lemma attains the rounded square-order bound.
  This concerns the maximum order of an abstract isotropic subgroup, not
  attainment by a selected design or a classification of subgroup types.
  Metabolicity does not assert a free or direct-summand Lagrangian.
- Realizability is claimed only for the elementary `(Z/2)^a` and `(Z/3)^b`
  targets on Steiner triple systems. Nonsquarefree exponents and general
  defect-group realizability are out of scope.
- The explicit selected-design deductions are described in the introduction
  and claim ledger. Their originality is not certified by the limited source
  search, and no firstness claim follows from failing to find an equivalent.
  The classical inputs are the work of their
  authors (Wilson; Caggegi–Falcone–Pavone; Chandler–Sin–Xiang; Ringe; Wall;
  Nikulin; Hanani; Hiss; Assmus–Key; Solé) and are used only as attributed prior
  art.
- No specific externally posed open question, including the strong-additivity
  questions in the related literature, is claimed to be resolved.
- The finite replay checks four concrete designs. It does not verify the
  general proof chain or the asymptotic realization theorem. The AI-assisted
  review is not independent specialist endorsement; see `AI_USE.md`.
- The paper carries no electrical, network, or Smith-chart interpretation; the
  "higher-star" terminology refers to the star vector of a design.
